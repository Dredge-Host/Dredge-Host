Build a website without code, from $3 a month

Every plan includes Sitejet Builder. Pick a template, edit it right on the page, and publish it to your own domain, with free HTTPS and email included. No plugins to keep updated, no code to write.

How it works

  1. Open the builder

    In cPanel, go to Domains → Sitejet Builder, click Create a New Website, and choose your domain.

  2. Pick a template

    Every template can be changed completely, or you can start from a blank page.

  3. Make it yours

    Drag and drop text, images, and sections, and edit everything right on the page.

  4. Publish

    Click Publish and your site goes live on your domain. Change it and publish again whenever you like.

What you get

Templates to start from

Choose a design that's close to what you want, then change any part of it.

Drag-and-drop editing

Move sections around and edit text and images where they sit. What you see is what your visitors get.

Very little upkeep

There are no plugins or themes to update, so a finished site keeps running without regular maintenance.

Your own domain

Publish to yourdomain.com or a subdomain like shop.yourdomain.com. Domains are $14/yr for a .com, or bring one you already own.

Free HTTPS

Every domain gets a certificate automatically, so your site shows the padlock with nothing to buy or set up.

Email on your domain

Set up you@yourdomain.com mailboxes on the same plan, at no extra cost.
