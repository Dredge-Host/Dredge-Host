About Dredge Host

Dredge Host is independent hosting for people who just want their site to be fast and up, without the enterprise sprawl or the dark patterns.

Why "Dredge"?

A dredge is the machine that clears a waterway so everything downstream can move freely. That's the whole idea behind this company. Hosting should pull the muck out of the way, the slow disks, the surprise invoices, the support mazes, so your site has a clear path to the people who need it.

What we believe

Most hosting problems aren't technical, they're about trust. You shouldn't need a decoder ring to understand your bill, or a week of emails to get a straight answer. We'd rather do a few things honestly than a hundred things with fine print.

Real limits, plain numbers

SSD storage and quotas stated in plain numbers on every plan, never "unlimited".

Honest by design

Clear pricing, a real status page, and plain-language answers when something goes wrong.

Human-sized

You reach the people who run the servers, not a ticket queue that forgets you overnight.

Who runs it

Dredge Host was started in 2026 and is run from New York by a small, hands-on team, so the people who build and maintain the platform are the same ones who answer when you email. No outsourced support desk, no ticket black hole.

Ruby Vataj Founder & CEO

What we actually run on

Dredge Host runs on a managed cPanel platform in a Virginia datacenter, with every account provisioned, tuned, and supported by us. It's shared hosting, run carefully and sold honestly.

Where we are now

Dredge Host is up and running as an independent company. We're not backed by a hyperscaler, and we have no ambition to be the biggest host on the internet. If that sounds like your kind of hosting company, pick a plan or ask us a question. To see how we stack up against bigger hosts, read Why Dredge.
