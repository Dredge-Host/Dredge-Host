About

A small shop that clears the channel.

Dredge Host is independent hosting for people who just want their site to be fast and up, without the enterprise sprawl or the dark patterns.

Why "Dredge"?

A dredge is the machine that clears a waterway so everything downstream can move freely. That's the whole idea behind this company. Hosting should pull the muck out of the way, the slow disks, the surprise invoices, the support mazes, so your site has a clear path to the people who need it.

What we believe

Most hosting problems aren't technical, they're about trust. You shouldn't need a decoder ring to understand your bill, or a week of emails to get a straight answer. We'd rather do a few things honestly than a hundred things with fine print.

Real limits, published

SSD storage and quotas stated in plain numbers on every plan, never "unlimited".

Honest by design

Clear pricing, published limits, a real status page, and plain-language answers when something goes wrong.

Human-sized

You reach the people who run the servers, not a ticket queue that forgets you overnight.

Who runs it

Dredge Host is run by a small, hands-on team, so the people who build and maintain the platform are the same ones who answer when you email. No outsourced support desk, no ticket black hole.

What we actually run on

We don't own datacenters and we're not going to imply that we do. Dredge Host runs on a managed cPanel platform leased from an established US host, in a US datacenter, with our accounts provisioned, tuned, and supported by us. It's shared hosting, run carefully and sold honestly.

Every plan's limits are published on the pricing page instead of "unlimited" and a fair use policy nobody reads.

Where we are now

Dredge Host is up and running as an independent operation, with no ambition to be the biggest host on the internet. We keep the company deliberately small so the people who run the platform are the same people who answer your email.
