What you get

One-click install

WordPress Manager in cPanel sets up WordPress, its database, and your admin login in a couple of minutes, with a one-click button to log in afterwards.

Automatic updates

Turn on automatic updates in WordPress Manager to keep WordPress current. Out-of-date plugins are the most common way sites get hacked.

Free HTTPS

Certificates are issued and renewed automatically for every domain and subdomain, so your site gets the padlock with nothing to buy or install.

Any plugin or theme

Shops, booking systems, page builders, forms: install what you need on every plan, with no premium tier to unlock plugins.

Your choice of PHP version

Switch PHP versions in about thirty seconds from cPanel when a plugin or theme needs a particular one.

Backups your way

Download your files and database any time, or add daily off-site backups with one-click restore from $7.50/yr.
