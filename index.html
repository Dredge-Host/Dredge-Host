What every plan includes

SSD storage

Every account sits on solid-state storage in a Virginia datacenter, so database queries and page loads aren't waiting on a spinning disk.

Your own cPanel

File Manager, MySQL, phpMyAdmin, cron jobs, PHP version switching, and SFTP. It's the standard control panel, so there's nothing proprietary to learn and nothing to unlearn if you leave.

One-click installs and a site builder

Install WordPress and a few hundred other apps from Softaculous, or build a site visually with the drag-and-drop Sitejet Builder. No code or config files needed.

Free HTTPS

Certificates are issued automatically for every domain and subdomain you add, and they renew on their own.

Email on your domain

Mailboxes, forwarders, and webmail for every domain on your account. Or point your MX records somewhere else; that's up to you.

Support by email

No chatbot and no outsourced first line. Your email goes to the people who built and run the platform.

Why "Dredge"?

A dredge clears a channel so everything downstream can move. That's the job here: get the slow disks, surprise invoices, and support mazes out of the way so your site can get on with it.

We're a small independent company. We run a managed cPanel platform in a Virginia datacenter and look after every account on it ourselves, so whoever answers your email knows how your account is set up. More about us.
