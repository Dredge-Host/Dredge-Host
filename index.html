Capacity

The whole platform, in three numbers.

Most hosts hide this. Dredge runs on one cPanel platform with fixed limits, and every plan we sell is carved out of it. When the pool is spoken for, we stop selling and start a waitlist instead of quietly cramming more accounts on.

80 GB SSD

Total storage across every account on the platform, ours included. Your plan's quota is a real reservation out of that pool.

1 TB transfer

Total outbound traffic per month, shared across everyone. We watch it weekly and close signups long before the platform starts to feel it.

25 accounts

The hard ceiling on customers. Number twenty-six waits until somebody leaves, and we'd rather tell you that than oversell.

About

Why "Dredge"?

A dredge clears the channel so everything downstream can move. That's the job: pull the muck out of the way, the slow disks, the surprise invoices, the support mazes, so your site has a clear path to the people who need it.

We're a small independent shop, not a hyperscaler. We lease a managed cPanel platform from an established US host and look after the accounts on it ourselves, which is exactly why the limits above are published rather than implied. Read our story.
