When Dredge isn't the right fit

We'd rather tell you now than after you've paid. Look elsewhere if you need:

  • Root access or a custom server stack. Dredge is managed shared hosting. A VPS is the better choice.
  • Windows or .NET hosting. Our servers run Linux.
  • Phone or 24/7 live chat support. We support customers by email.
  • Servers outside the US. Everything runs in our Virginia datacenter.

If none of those apply, Dredge is probably a good home for your site. Compare plans or ask us a question first.

Need more than 300 GB of transfer a month? Email support@dredgehost.com and we'll put together a custom plan.
