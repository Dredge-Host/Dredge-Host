Any time, in both directions, from your client area. Upgrades take effect immediately and you pay the prorated difference; downgrades credit the difference toward future invoices. No phone call, no retention gauntlet.

You get an email, not an invoice. Storage is a hard quota, so you'll be asked to clear space or move up a plan before it becomes a problem. Transfer we look at over the month: a one-off spike is fine and we absorb it, but if you're consistently over the line we'll help you move to the plan that actually fits.

A managed cPanel platform leased from an established US host, in a US datacenter, with our accounts provisioned and supported by us. It's shared hosting and we're not going to dress it up as anything else — what you're buying is a carefully sized slice of it and somebody who answers email.

The platform takes weekly backups and we can restore an account from those. You can also generate a full cPanel backup of your own whenever you like and keep it off-site. For anything you can't afford to lose, do that too — any host that tells you their backups are enough on their own is overselling.
