Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Dredge Host ("we", "us") is a small, independent web hosting company. This policy explains what we collect and what we do with it. It's written to be read, not to hide behind. If anything here is unclear, email us.

What we collect

We collect what you give us in order to provide the service:

  • Account information (name, email) when you sign up.
  • Name, email, and message, if you use the contact form.

We do not run advertising trackers or sell data. Billing is handled by our payment processor; we don't store full card numbers on our servers.

How we use it

  • To create and manage your account and provide the hosting service.
  • To reply to your message (if you contacted us).
  • To send occasional important updates, which you can opt out of at any time.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We don't send spam. We don't share your address with advertisers. One launch email is one launch email.

Where it's stored

Your information is stored with the service providers we use to run our mailing list and website. We keep it only as long as needed for the purpose you gave it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email using the link in that email, or by emailing support@dredgehost.com. You can also request a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking cookies. If we add privacy-friendly analytics later, we'll note it here first.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know by email.

This document is a plain-language starting point and not legal advice. Have a professional review it before you rely on it for compliance in your jurisdiction.

Contact
