Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Dredge collects only what it needs to run your hosting account and bill for it. We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything unrelated to the service.

What we collect

  • Account and billing details: your name, email address, billing address, and phone number (if you give one), plus your order and invoice history.
  • Payment information: card payments are handled by Stripe. We see the card type and last four digits, never the full number.
  • Support conversations: the emails you send us and our replies.
  • Technical logs: IP addresses and timestamps from logins to your client area and cPanel, and standard server logs for your sites. These help us keep the platform secure and fix problems.
  • Your hosted content: the files, databases, and email on your account. We don't look through it, except when needed to investigate an abuse report, fix a problem you've asked us about, or comply with the law.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run Dredge:

  • Stripe, to process payments.
  • Our infrastructure provider, an established US hosting company whose servers store your account and data.
  • Law enforcement or courts, only when we're legally required to, such as by a valid subpoena or court order.

Cookies

The client area at my.dredgehost.com uses cookies to keep you logged in and to remember your cart. dredgehost.com itself doesn't use cookies or analytics. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

How long we keep it

We keep account details while your account is active. After an account is closed, its hosted files, databases, and email are deleted within 30 days. Billing records such as invoices and payments are kept for as long as tax law requires, usually up to 7 years.

Your choices

You can update your details in your client area at any time. To get a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email support@dredgehost.com. We'll do it, except for records we're legally required to keep.

Security

Logins and all pages on dredgehost.com and my.dredgehost.com are served over HTTPS, and access to customer data is limited to the person who runs Dredge. No system is perfectly secure, but if we ever learn of a breach that affects your data, we'll tell you promptly.

Children

Dredge is for people 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If we make a meaningful change to this policy, we'll email active customers before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to support@dredgehost.com.
