Back up and restore your site

The platform takes weekly backups, but the only backup you fully control is one you've downloaded yourself. Here's how.

What's already backed up

Dredge takes a weekly backup of every account. If something goes badly wrong, email support@dredgehost.com and we can restore from it. But a week is a long time for a busy site, so keep your own copies too.

Download a backup

In cPanel, open Backup. You'll see two kinds:

  • Partial backups are what you want day to day. Download your Home Directory (all your files) and each MySQL Database separately. They're quick and you can restore them yourself.
  • Full Account Backup bundles everything, including email and settings, into one file. It's mainly for moving to another host, and it can only be restored by us.

Store your downloads somewhere other than your computer as well, such as an external drive or cloud storage.

Restore from your own backup

  1. In cPanel, open Backup.
  2. Under Restore a Home Directory Backup or Restore a MySQL Database, choose the file you downloaded and click Upload.

A restore overwrites what's there now. If you only need one lost file, it's safer to open the backup on your computer and upload just that file with File Manager.

Mind your storage

A Full Account Backup is created inside your account before you download it, so it counts toward your plan's storage. If you're near your limit, it may fail. Stick to partial backups, and delete any backup file left in your home folder once it's downloaded.

For WordPress sites

WordPress Manager in cPanel can take and restore backups of a single WordPress site with one click, and can schedule them automatically. Download a copy now and then so you're not relying only on copies stored in the same account.
