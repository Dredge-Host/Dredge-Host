Manage DNS records

Add or edit A, CNAME, MX, and TXT records in cPanel's Zone Editor — for things like domain verification, external email, or pointing a subdomain at another service.

On this page

  1. When you need this
  2. Open the Zone Editor
  3. Add a record
  4. Edit or delete a record
  5. How long changes take

When you need this

If your domain's nameservers point to Dredge (the default setup from the Connect a domain guide), then cPanel manages your DNS. You'll need the Zone Editor when:

  • A third-party service asks you to add a TXT record for verification (Google Search Console, Mailgun, etc.).
  • You want to point a subdomain at an external service with a CNAME.
  • You're switching email to an external provider and need to change MX records.

Open the Zone Editor

In cPanel, open Zone Editor. Click Manage next to the domain you want to edit.

Add a record

  1. Click Add Record and choose the type.
  2. Fill in the fields the service gave you. Common types:
TypeWhat it doesExample use
APoints a name to an IPv4 addressPointing your domain at a server
CNAMEPoints a name to another namePointing shop.yourdomain.com at Shopify
MXTells email where to goRouting email through Google Workspace
TXTHolds text dataDomain verification, SPF, DKIM, DMARC

Edit or delete a record

Find the record in the list and click Edit or Delete. Be careful with records you didn't create yourself — some are needed for your site and email to work.

How long changes take

Changes made here usually take effect within a few minutes, but some ISPs cache DNS for up to 24 hours. If you're testing, try flushing your local DNS cache or using a different network.
