Sitejet Builder or WordPress: which should you use?

Every Dredge plan includes a drag-and-drop site builder and one-click WordPress. Here's how to choose between them.

The short version

Every Dredge plan includes both. Sitejet Builder is a drag-and-drop editor for building a site visually. WordPress is a full content management system with thousands of plugins. The right one depends on what your site needs to do.

How they compare

 Sitejet BuilderWordPress
How you buildPick a template and edit it on the pageChoose a theme, then add pages and posts in an editor
Learning curveGentleModerate
Extra featuresWhat the builder includesThousands of plugins: shops, bookings, memberships, forms
BloggingBasicExcellent
UpkeepVery littleRegular updates to WordPress, plugins, and themes

Choose Sitejet Builder if

  • You want a good-looking brochure site, portfolio, or landing page quickly.
  • You'd rather not manage updates or plugins.
  • Your site won't grow much beyond a handful of pages.

Choose WordPress if

  • You plan to blog regularly or publish lots of content.
  • You need features like a shop, bookings, or member logins.
  • You want the widest choice of designs and developers who can help.

How to start

Open cPanel and look for Sitejet Builder to start with a template, or follow Install WordPress in one click. You can try one and switch later; nothing is locked in.
