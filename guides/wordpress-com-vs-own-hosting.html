WordPress.com vs. WordPress on your own hosting

They share a name but they're different products. Here's what changes when you run WordPress on your own hosting instead of on WordPress.com.

On this page

  1. The short version
  2. How they compare
  3. Choose WordPress.com if
  4. Choose your own hosting if
  5. Already on WordPress.com?

The short version

WordPress.com is a hosted service that runs WordPress for you, with the most technical parts locked away. WordPress on your own hosting, like a Dredge plan, is the same free software installed on your account, where you control everything: plugins, themes, files, email, and your database.

How they compare

 WordPress.comWordPress on your own hosting
SetupSign up and start writingOne-click install from cPanel, about two minutes
Plugins and custom themesLimited to higher-priced plansInstall any you like on every plan
Email on your domainUsually sold separatelyIncluded: 10 to 75 mailboxes, depending on plan
Files and databaseNo direct access on most plansFull access through cPanel, FTP, and phpMyAdmin
Other sites and appsWordPress onlyRun other apps, static sites, or subdomains alongside it
Updates and securityHandled for youYou click Update; we look after the server

Choose WordPress.com if

  • You only want a blog or simple site and never expect to add plugins.
  • You'd rather never see an update button.
  • You don't need email on your domain, or you already pay for it elsewhere.

Choose your own hosting if

  • You want a specific plugin, like a booking system, shop, or form builder.
  • You want email on your domain included in the price.
  • You might add a second site, a subdomain, or a non-WordPress app later.
  • You want to be able to download everything and move hosts whenever you like.
